Case study · Jamaica

A national web platform: enhanced, adopted and supported

For Jamaica's Ministry of Education, we improved the platform that runs school inspections across the country, delivered a new public website and trained the people who use them. We then provided system support and website security updates.

The challenge

Moving a national process off paper

Jamaica's school inspections relied heavily on paper. Inspectors filled in forms on site, and reports were compiled by hand and published late. The Ministry had no central data and no quick way to see what was happening across its schools.

It needed a web platform to handle the whole process, from scheduling visits to publishing reports, and it needed inspectors and school leaders across the country to actually use it.

What we did

Advice, build, adoption and support from one team

01

Understand and design

Systems consultancy and data architecture: working with the Inspectorate to map how inspections really ran and what the platform had to do.

02

Build

Enhancements to the national web platform for managing, reporting and publishing inspections, plus a new public website.

03

Adopt

Stakeholder training, change management, communications planning and social media, so a national workforce took the new system on.

04

Support

System support and website security updates, keeping the platform running safely after launch.

The result

One platform for inspections across the country

School inspections across Jamaica are managed, reported and published through a single platform, covering 954 schools and around 20,000 teachers. We stayed on after launch to support and secure it, so the platform kept working for the people who rely on it.
