Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect personal information, and the rights you have.

Who we are

Innovation 2 Solution Limited ("I2S", "we") is the data controller for personal information collected through this website and in the course of our consultancy work. We are a company registered in England and Wales, company number 15126741, registered office 2 Wheeleys Road, Edgbaston, Birmingham B15 2LD.

Contact us about anything in this policy at enquiries@innovation2solution.com or by post to the address above.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, work email, organisation, area of interest, approximate budget and message when you use our contact form or email us.
  • Client and supplier records: business contact details and correspondence needed to deliver and manage a contract.
  • Website analytics: if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site, such as pages visited, device and approximate location. See our Cookie Policy.
  • Cookie preferences: your consent choice, stored in your own browser.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or steps you have asked us to take before entering a contract.
  • To deliver and administer consultancy contracts: performance of a contract.
  • To understand how the website is used: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through Cookie Settings in the footer.
  • To keep accounting and tax records: legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf under contract:

  • Formspree, which receives contact form submissions and forwards them to us.
  • Google, which provides Google Analytics (only if you consent).
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Cloudflare, which routes email sent to our addresses, and Google (Gmail), where we receive and reply to it.

We may also share information with professional advisers, or where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK, it is protected by UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge where the provider is certified) or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: 2 years after our last contact.
  • Client contract and financial records: 6 years after the end of the contract.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics keeps detailed event data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email us to make a request; we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date below.
