About us

Senior people. Bespoke solutions. Straight answers.

Innovation 2 Solution is a boutique technical consultancy and development house. We advise on technology, then design, build and support the software our clients need. You work directly with a small team of senior industry professionals, with specialist developers and associates brought in when a project needs them.

Who we are

Small by design, experienced where it counts

Innovation 2 Solution has been trading since 2015. The current company, Innovation 2 Solution Limited (Company No. 15126741), was incorporated in 2023 to continue the practice. We are based in Edgbaston, Birmingham, and work with clients in the UK and the Caribbean.

We are a group of senior industry professionals with many years' experience in IT leadership, platform strategy, governance, software delivery and change. Every engagement is led by a senior consultant. For larger projects we bring in specialist developers and associates, and we tell you who they are before work starts. No junior analysts, no hand-offs.

How we work

Three principles behind every engagement

Independent advice

Our recommendations come from your needs, not from a product we want to sell. We recommend off-the-shelf when it fits and bespoke when it doesn't, and we declare any interests before work starts.

Built to last

Clean, documented code, a proper handover and training for your people, so what we build keeps working and you're never locked in.

Straight answers

We tell clients what they need to hear. If a proposed approach isn't right, we say so, even when that means a smaller project for us.
