Cookie Policy

The cookies we use, why we use them, and how to change your choices.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. We also use your browser's local storage, which works in a similar way. This page explains what we use and how to control it.

What we use

NameCategoryPurposeDuration
i2s_cookie_consentStrictly necessaryRemembers your cookie choices (stored in your browser)Until you clear it or change your choice
_ga, _ga_CPJ8XCB3KEAnalytics (only with your consent)Google Analytics: tells us which pages are visited and how people find the siteUp to 2 years

We do not currently use functionality or marketing cookies. If that changes, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.

Your choices

Analytics cookies are off until you accept them. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie Settings, which is also in the footer of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

For more about how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.
