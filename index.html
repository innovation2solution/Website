What we do

From the right decision to working software

Most clients come to us with a problem, not a specification. We help shape the answer, then build it properly.

01

Technical consultancy

Independent IT strategy, architecture reviews, requirements and procurement support. We recommend off-the-shelf when it fits and bespoke when it doesn't.

02

Bespoke software, web and apps

Web platforms, portals and mobile apps designed around how your people actually work, from first prototype to production.

03

How we deliver

One team from discovery to long after launch

Projects stall when advice, build and support come from different suppliers. We do all three, so nothing gets lost between the plan and the people using the system.

  1. Understand: discovery workshops that pin down the real problem
  2. Design: architecture, user experience and a plan you can cost
  3. Build: working software in short cycles, with regular demos
  4. Deliver: testing, data migration and a managed launch
  5. Adopt: training and user support so people actually use it
  6. Improve: ongoing support and new features as your needs change

Featured project · Jamaica

A national web platform, enhanced, adopted and supported

For Jamaica's National Education Inspectorate, in a World Bank-supported reform programme, we enhanced the national web platform that manages, reports and publishes school inspections, delivered a new public website and trained the people who use them. We then provided system support and website security updates.
